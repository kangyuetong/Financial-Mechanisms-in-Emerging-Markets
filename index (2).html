09 — Methodology

How the model works

A transparent price-premium model, with observed market references separated from user-defined assumptions.

Calculation logic

Price premium
Maximum of zero and (SAF price − jet fuel price)
Certificate proceeds
Assumed certificate revenue × capture rate
Unfunded premium
Maximum of zero and (price premium − certificate proceeds − public support)
Annual illustrative financing need
Unfunded premium × nameplate capacity (at 100% utilisation)
Break-even certificate rate
Maximum of zero and (premium − public support), divided by capture rate

When capture is zero and public support does not cover the premium, certificate financing cannot close the gap. Excess funding is not treated as profit.
