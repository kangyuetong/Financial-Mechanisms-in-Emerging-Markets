# Financial-Mechanisms-in-Emerging-Markets

**Modelling Avelia's Book-and-Claim in SAF Markets**

Model developed for NUS FIN4751: Advanced Independent Study in Finance.

### [Explore the Interactive Financial Dashboard](https://kangyuetong.github.io/Financial-Mechanisms-in-Emerging-Markets/)

An interactive financial modelling platform exploring the role of book-and-claim mechanisms, certificate revenue, and public incentives in financing sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) adoption in emerging markets.

The dashboard allows users to explore SAF price premiums, compare financing mechanisms, assess project investment viability through NPV and IRR, and conduct sensitivity and scenario analyses.

**Note:** The dashboard combines published market benchmarks with illustrative financial assumptions. Results are intended for research and scenario exploration rather than investment decisions.
